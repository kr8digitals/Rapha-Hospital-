Who We Are

Welcome to Chi-Tom Rapha Hospital

Chi-Tom Rapha Hospital & Maternity is committed to making standard healthcare available, accessible and affordable to all citizens through a humane approach.

We provide a range of medical services including outpatient care, antenatal care, laboratory services, ultrasound, surgery, emergency care and immunization, with specialist care in Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Surgery and Paediatrics.

What We Offer

Our Services

A complete range of medical services — delivered with professionalism, compassion and dignity.

Outpatient Department

Medical consultations, assessment, treatment and follow-up care for patients who do not require hospital admission.

Antenatal Clinic

Professional antenatal care and monitoring to support mothers throughout pregnancy and prepare them for a safe and informed delivery journey.

Laboratory Services

Laboratory investigations that support clinical assessment, diagnosis and informed patient care.

Ultrasound

Ultrasound imaging to support clinical assessment and pregnancy-related monitoring where appropriate.

Surgeries

Surgical care provided with appropriate clinical assessment, professional judgement and attention to patient safety.

Immunization

Immunization services that support disease prevention and healthier individuals and families.
