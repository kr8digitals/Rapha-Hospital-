How This Dashboard Works

Everything you save here updates the live site immediately and for every visitor — posts, announcements, testimonials, phone numbers, addresses, even the hero message. Content is stored on the website’s server through the built-in content API, so what you see here is exactly what the public sees. Use Backup & Restore to export a JSON file of your content: keep it safe, or import it to restore the entire site anytime.

Quick Start

1. Write a new article under Health Insights (plain text with simple markdown: ## heading, - list, > callout, **bold**).
2. Post an Announcement for anything visitors should see on the homepage.
3. If your phone or address changes, update it once in Site Settings — it updates everywhere on the site.
4. Hit Export in Backup & Restore to keep a copy.
