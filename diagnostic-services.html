Laboratory & Ultrasound — Answers You Can Rely On

Good medicine is built on good information. Our diagnostic services are on campus, supporting clinical decisions at any hour of the day or night. Families across Izzi and Abakaliki use our laboratory and ultrasound services for blood tests, scans and clinical monitoring.

On-Campus Diagnostics

The Information Behind Good Decisions

When your laboratory and imaging are in the same building as the team treating you, care moves faster and stays connected.

Laboratory Services

Blood tests and laboratory investigations that underpin everyday clinical care — from monitoring your general health to supporting pregnancy care, surgical assessment and emergency decisions.

Ultrasound Imaging

Ultrasound is a safe, radiation-free way to look inside the body. It is central to pregnancy monitoring and helps with many other clinical questions across the hospital.

Safe · Radiation-Free
Why It Matters

One Campus, One Complete Picture

A doctor reviewing your result is not waiting on a referral across town — the whole picture is in front of them. For expectant mothers, this is why we recommend completing your antenatal journey here: the scans and the check-ups are part of one continuous story.

  • Supports antenatal monitoring, surgical planning and emergency care.
  • Available as part of our 24/7 hospital services.
  • Interpreted by the clinical team who will act on the result.
  • Simple booking by phone or through our consultation form.

You can simply call or book a consultation and tell us what you need — the clinical team will advise on the right investigations for your situation.

Ultrasound is one of the safest imaging methods available and is a standard part of antenatal care. Read our explanation in “What Do Ultrasound Scans Show in Pregnancy?”.
